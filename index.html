Privacy Policy

At Shop MODISHA, accessible through our express PWA web portal, we are committed to maintaining the trust and confidence of our customers. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When placing an order using our web form, we require specific details necessary for completing your transaction, including your Full Name, list of shopping items, budget/cash amount, and preferred payment method.

2. How Your Information Is Processed

Your input data is formatted directly on your device into a pre-structured message sent straight to our WhatsApp business line (0824598656). We do not store your private submission data on external databases or sell your information to third-party advertisers.

3. Communication

We only use your provided contact information (WhatsApp number and provided name) to confirm order fulfillment, total prices, and schedule the delivery to your location.

4. Data Security & Storage

WhatsApp provides end-to-end encryption for chat communications. Offline form drafts are stored strictly on your local browser cache and are never shared.

Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions govern all sales and delivery services provided by Shop MODISHA.

A standard delivery fee of R5 applies to all dispatched orders. This fee is automatically noted on your WhatsApp order breakdown and will be added to the final order invoice upon fulfillment.
